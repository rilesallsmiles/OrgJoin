05How I work

Diagnose. Design. Install.

01

Diagnose

I uncover the operational seams: where decisions stall, information breaks, incentives conflict, and leadership is compensating with heroics. You get a plain-language map of where execution breaks.

02

Design

I recommend the smallest mechanism that fits your stage: roles, decision rights, workflows, metrics, and operating cadence. You decide what to adopt.

03

Install

I help build the changes and shift or remove tasks from titles, working alongside your team until it holds without me.

The CEO decides. I don’t make decisions for you, I don’t write a deck and disappear, and I don’t stay forever.

06Why I’m here

Hi, I’m Riley. I designed myself out of my last job.

I was the Senior Director of Membership and Operations at Newlab. Part of the job was finding where the organization was straining and fixing it, so I built a strategy to do that and then ran it.

It worked well enough that my own role stopped being necessary. It was the right move for the company, and I’m glad I made it. It’s still a strange feeling to finish your own job.

It’s also how I think about this work: good operating design shouldn’t need a hero in the middle of it. If it only works while you’re standing in it, you haven’t fixed it. You’ve just become the joint.

Organizational Joinery is new, and you’d be one of my first clients. I’d rather say that plainly than dress it up. I’m a mechanical engineer turned operator with an MBA, and I’ve spent my career inside the companies where coordination breaks first.
